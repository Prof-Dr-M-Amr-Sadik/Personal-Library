Prof. Dr. M. Amr Sadik

For decades, Prof. Dr. M. Amr Sadik has worked where people and strategy meet: in executive suites across the Middle East and the Gulf, in lecture halls from Paris to Latvia, and in the journals and magazines that shape how leaders think about work.

This library gathers that work in one place: research on AI, culture and rebuilding after crisis; six books, among them readings of HR through Sun Tzu, Dante and the Godfather; and a long line of articles that asked HR to stop supporting the organization and start shaping it. Every spine on this shelf is a question he asked about how we work.

  • Co-founder and Vice President of Educators, Dimensions Consulting Ltd, UK
  • Adjunct Professor of HR and Strategy Management, IPE Management School, Paris
  • HR Professor, GUST, Latvia
  • Member of the HBR Advisory Council
  • Ranked among the Top 50 Thought HR Leaders by Thinkers360, 2024
  • Winner of multiple global HR awards
